Why we serve

Service is why we exist

Alpha Phi Omega was founded on service, and it remains our first commitment. Every semester, brothers give their time to causes on campus, in Storrs, and beyond, because we believe a strong community is one we build together.

Across the country, APO provides more service on more campuses than any other collegiate service organization.
