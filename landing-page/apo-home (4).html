Who we are

Leaders, friends, and volunteers since 1925

Alpha Phi Omega is a gender-inclusive national service fraternity, founded by Frank Reed Horton at Lafayette College on December 16, 1925, on principles drawn from the Scout Oath and Law. Brothers give their time to the fraternity, the campus, the community, and the nation, and build leadership skills and lifelong friendships along the way.

Our Delta Sigma chapter was chartered at UConn on January 12, 1947. Since 1970, we’ve also cared for UConn’s live mascot, Jonathan the Husky.
